title: "Project Tracker: dependencies, comments, notifications, and ownership transfer"
summary: "The Project Tracker is now complete. Phases and activities can depend on one another with a visual diagram and unmet-prerequisite flags, members can discuss items with @mentions, email notifications go out on the events that matter, and project ownership can be handed over."
new:
  - title: "Dependencies with a visual diagram"
    detail: "Mark any phase or activity as depending on one or more others in the same project. The new Diagram tab draws the whole graph with prerequisites to the left of what they block, and an amber dashed connector wherever a prerequisite is not yet Completed. Circular dependencies are rejected, and a phase cannot depend on its own activity."
  - title: "Unmet dependency flags"
    detail: "Any phase or activity with outstanding prerequisites now shows an amber \"N unmet\" badge in the item list, with a tooltip naming what it is waiting on. The flag clears automatically once the prerequisite is Completed, without changing the dependent item's own status."
  - title: "Comments with @mentions"
    detail: "Discuss any phase or activity in the new Comments tab. Type @ to mention someone — only people with access to the project appear in the picker and can be tagged. Viewers can comment even though they cannot change project content."
  - title: "Email notifications"
    detail: "Members are emailed when a phase or activity status changes, when a new activity is created, and when a comment is posted. Each email names the project, the affected item, the action, who did it, and links straight to the project."
  - title: "Ownership transfer"
    detail: "A Project Owner can hand ownership to an existing Editor. The new Owner gains full permission management, the previous Owner becomes an Editor, and both are emailed. Transferring to a Viewer is blocked with a prompt to grant Editor access first."
improved:
  - title: "One email per event, not one per person"
    detail: "A single notification covers everyone who needs it rather than sending a separate message to each recipient, and you are never emailed about your own action. Re-saving a status without changing it, or editing only an item's name or dates, sends nothing."
  - title: "Warning before deleting something others depend on"
    detail: "Deleting a phase or activity that other items list as a prerequisite now warns you first and names the affected items. Those items keep the reference and continue to show it as unmet until it is updated or the item is restored."
