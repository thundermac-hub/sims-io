version: "2.28.0"
date: "2026-08-24"
title: "Pick a mapped outlet on flagged tickets"
summary: "A ticket that needs an outlet match now offers the outlets its contact is already mapped to in a dropdown — covering both several mapped outlets and a franchise-wide mapping — instead of asking for the franchise and outlet ids by hand."
new:
  - title: "Select the outlet from the contact's own mappings"
    detail: "A Respond.io ticket auto-links only when its contact resolves to exactly one outlet; every other case landed on the outlet-link panel asking for a typed FID and OID even though SIMS already knew which outlets that contact represents. The panel now lists them. A contact mapped to several outlets offers those outlets, and a contact mapped franchise-wide offers every outlet under that franchise, each shown by name alongside its id. Picking one fills the franchise and outlet ids together, so a contact mapped across more than one franchise can no longer be linked to the wrong one."
improved:
  - title: "Manual ids stay available as the fallback"
    detail: "A contact with no usable mapping still gets the franchise and outlet id fields, and an \"Enter ids manually\" toggle covers the case where the right outlet is not mapped to the contact yet. When the ticket's franchise was pre-filled from a franchise-wide mapping, the choice list is narrowed to that franchise so the pre-fill cannot be contradicted."
